# Candidate rule: write for a reader who did not see the work

2.2.1 has no rule about labels a writer coins while working: step
numbers, hypothesis names, "option B", ticket shorthand, abbreviations.
The candidate adds one bullet to "Write this way", directly after "Lead
with what the reader came for":

- Write for a reader who did not see the work. A label you made up
  along the way (a step number, "option B", "fix-2", an abbreviation
  from your notes) means nothing to them. Say what it stands for, or
  define it once where it first appears. A name the reader already uses
  (a file, a function, a ticket they filed) stays.

Nothing else in `SKILL.md` differs between the two files the writers
are given.
