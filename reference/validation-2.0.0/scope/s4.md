The super let me in without asking for a badge, which told me how long the smell of old coffee had been drifting down the hallway. Apartment 4C was tidy in the way a place is when the person has stopped living in it: a mug on the drainboard, a jacket over the chair, a phone charger plugged in with nothing on the end. Four days, the landlord had said. I stood in the doorway until my eyes stopped looking for what I expected and started looking for what was out of place. The calendar by the fridge was turned to the right month. Tuesday had a circle drawn around it, and nothing written inside.
