# Candidate rule: lead with what the reader came for

2.1.0 has no rule about the order of a piece or about leaving out true
facts the reader will not use. The candidate adds one bullet to "Write
this way", directly after "Say each thing once":

- Lead with what the reader came for. In a reply, message, PR
  description, status update, or report, the first sentence or two give
  the answer, the decision, or what you need from them, and the support
  follows. Leave out what this reader will not use, even when it is true
  and you have it.

Nothing else in `SKILL.md` differs between the two files the writers
are given.
